02 · Findings

Differences, listed — not just screenshots

When the channels finish loading, Isoline compares them and lists what differs. Prices are extracted from structured data and converted at the daily ECB reference rate, so a 9 % gap shows up as a number.

  4. Headers and CDNHeader diff by region, Vary, cache and edge node.

04 · Device tunnel (VPN)

A system-wide VPN tunnel, named as one

Isoline includes a device tunnel (VPN). It routes all traffic of your device through one point of presence you configured — to test other apps from a region, or simply to use a configuration you already have.

  • Off by default. You turn it on in the Tunnel tab, after an explanation screen and the system VPN permission.
  • Traffic goes to your chosen server only. We operate no servers and never see it.
  • Routing rules, connect on demand (iPhone, Mac) and per-app routing (Android).

09 · Questions

Frequently asked

Is Isoline a VPN service?

No. Isoline is a testing tool and a client. It contains a device tunnel (VPN), but we do not provide servers, accounts or subscriptions. You connect only to points of presence you configured yourself.

Where do I get a configuration?

From your own server, from your company's administrator, or from a provider you already have an agreement with. Isoline accepts share links, subscriptions, QR codes and files. See Importing.

What data do you collect?

None. Points, checks, reports and settings are stored only on your device. Requests go to the points you chose and to a few public services used to measure the exit (for example Cloudflare). Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it cost anything?

Isoline has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Any costs of points of presence are between you and your provider.

Is it legal to use?

Isoline is a general-purpose network and testing tool. You are responsible for using it, and the configurations you add, in line with the laws of your country and the terms of the services you test. See the Terms of Use.

When can I download it?

Isoline is being prepared for the App Store and Google Play. Store links will appear on this page when the apps are published.
