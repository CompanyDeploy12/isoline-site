In short

  • We collect nothing through the app. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. We have no servers that carry your traffic or store your data.
  • Your data stays on your device. Points of presence, subscriptions, checks, snapshots, reports and settings are stored locally; access keys are kept in the Keychain (Apple) or encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Traffic goes where you send it. Checks and the device tunnel (VPN) go to the points of presence you added. Their operators can see that traffic, just like an internet provider. We cannot.
  • A few public services are used for measurements (Cloudflare, Google Public DNS, the European Central Bank, and IPinfo only with your own token). They see the IP address of the point you use — or yours, when you check without a point.
  • A device identifier is sent only if you allow it, per subscription, and only to that subscription's provider.
  • You can erase everything in Settings › Data › Erase all data, or by deleting the app. See Data deletion.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Isoline apps for iPhone, Mac and Android (the “app”) and to this website. It does not apply to services of third parties that you connect to through the app — for example the servers (“points of presence”) and subscription providers you add, or the websites you check. Those parties are independent controllers with their own privacy policies.

3. What we receive: nothing from the app

The app does not require or offer an account. It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or other data-collecting third-party SDKs. We do not receive your points of presence, configurations, checked addresses, findings, snapshots, reports, logs, device tunnel traffic or IP address. There is no server operated by us that the app talks to.

The only personal data we process ourselves is:

  • When you contact us (e-mail or phone): your contact details and the content of your message, to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns using the app, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep this correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Statistics from the app stores, if you allowed them. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics or diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports. They do not identify you to us, and we use them only to find and fix errors. Apple and Google process this data as independent controllers under their own policies; you can turn it off in your device settings.

5. What leaves your device, and to whom

5.1 Points of presence you chose

When you run a check, open a site in a channel, run diagnostics or turn on the device tunnel, the traffic goes through the point of presence you selected. Its operator — you, your employer or your provider — can see this traffic and your IP address, as an internet provider can. We have no relationship with these operators and receive nothing from them.

5.2 Subscription providers

When you add or refresh a subscription, the app downloads it from the address you entered. The provider receives a normal HTTPS request: the IP address it comes from, User-Agent: Isoline/<version> (<system> <version>) or your own value, and any login details contained in the URL. By default no device identifiers are sent. The app does not obey provider instructions that would hide settings, connect automatically or silently change the subscription address.

For the baseline channel “Here” and for diagnostics without a point, the request is direct and the service sees your IP address. The answers are processed on your device; we never receive them. Each service acts under its own privacy policy.

5.4 Websites you check

The sites you open in channels receive what any browser sends: the IP address of the point (or yours for “Here”), the user agent and language you set for the channel, cookies of that session. Channel sessions are isolated from each other. WebRTC in channels is off by default.

5.5 What you share yourself

Exports (PDF, ZIP, JSON), backups, shared points, QR codes and shared logs leave the device only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. Exports never contain access keys; IP addresses can be masked. When you share the diagnostic log, the app removes UUIDs, passwords and keys first.

6. Optional install identifier (device binding)

Some subscription providers bind a subscription to a limited number of devices and return only placeholder entries until the app identifies the device. In that case the app explains the request and asks you. Only if you tap “Allow and load” (or turn on “Send device identifier” for that subscription) does the app send, to that provider only, when it downloads that subscription:

  • x-hwid: a random identifier of this installation (a UUID generated by the app — not a serial number, not derived from hardware, not the advertising ID, IDFV or Android ID);
  • x-device-os (iOS, macOS or Android), x-ver-os (system version) and x-device-model (generic model such as “iPhone”, “Mac” or the Android model name).

The choice is per subscription and can be turned off at any time. Settings › Privacy › Install identifier shows whether an identifier exists and to how many subscriptions it is sent, and lets you reset it. “Erase all data” deletes it. The legal basis for the provider's processing is your choice to use its service; we do not receive the identifier.

7. Device tunnel (VPN)

The app contains a system-wide VPN tunnel, built with Apple's Network Extension framework (Packet Tunnel Provider) on iPhone and Mac and with Android's VpnService. It is off by default and runs only after you turn it on in the Tunnel tab, confirm an explanation screen and accept the system VPN permission.

  • While it is on, your device's traffic (or the apps and destinations you selected in routing rules) is encrypted according to the protocol of your configuration and sent to the point of presence you chose.
  • The tunnel runs entirely on your device. We operate no VPN servers, do not see this traffic and keep no connection or activity logs — there is nothing on our side to log.
  • The operator of your point can see the traffic it forwards. Choose operators you trust.
  • The Live Activity, widgets and the Android notification show only the tunnel state on your device.

8. Device permissions

  • VPN configuration — to create the device tunnel, only when you turn it on.
  • Camera — to scan a QR code with a configuration. Decoding happens on the device; camera images are not stored or sent. Picking a QR image from Photos uses the system picker and needs no photo-library access.
  • Notifications (Android) — for the ongoing tunnel notification with an “Off” button.
  • On Android, the per-app routing list reads the list of launchable apps on the device; it stays on the device.

The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, advertising identifier or files other than those you open.

9. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no fonts or scripts from other servers and no embedded content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, your browser sends the usual technical data (IP address, browser, requested page) to GitHub, which may log it for security and operation under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.

10. International transfers

We do not transfer personal data from the app to anyone. Points of presence, subscription providers and the services listed in section 5.3 may be located outside the European Economic Area; requests to them are made because you chose to use them. The website host GitHub, Inc. and the app stores of Apple and Google may process data in the United States under their own safeguards (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

11. How long data is kept

  • Data in the app stays on your device until you delete it, erase all data or delete the app. The diagnostic log is kept for 7 days.
  • Correspondence with us: as described in section 3.
  • Data held by points of presence, providers and services you used: according to their policies.

12. Data deletion

Because the app keeps your data only on your device, you delete it yourself — no request to us is needed:

  1. Erase everything in the app: Settings › Data › Erase all data. This deletes all points, subscriptions, reports, snapshots, the VPN configuration, the install identifier and settings. It cannot be undone.
  2. Delete individual items: swipe or use the delete action on a point, subscription or report.
  3. Reset the install identifier: Settings › Privacy › Install identifier › Reset identifier.
  4. Delete the app. On Android this removes all app data. On iPhone and Mac, deleting the app removes its data; items in the Keychain are removed by “Erase all data” — use it before deleting the app if you want to be sure.
  5. Data at third parties (your provider, the operator of a point) must be requested from them.

If you have written to us and want your correspondence deleted, write to vojkovic@conciergegroup.pro; we will delete it unless we are legally required to keep it.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we hold no app data about you, most requests will concern correspondence with us; write to vojkovic@conciergegroup.pro.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Croatia this is the Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), Zagreb, azop.hr. You may also contact the authority in your country of residence.

14. Children

Isoline is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process data of children.

15. Security

  • Access keys are stored in the system Keychain or encrypted with an Android Keystore key (AES-GCM); Android cloud backup is disabled.
  • Every import — including links from providers and websites — opens a preview that you confirm; nothing is imported silently.
  • Exports never include access keys; shared logs are redacted.
  • No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.
